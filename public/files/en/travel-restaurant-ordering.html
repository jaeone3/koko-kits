Koko Restaurant Ordering Kit

Say how many people and order food at a Korean restaurant.

Roles

RoleWhat they do
StaffSeats guests and asks for the order.
CustomerSays the party size and orders food.

Key Phrases

KoreanEnglishRomanization
두 명이요.Two people.Du myeong-iyo.
김치찌개 하나 주세요.One kimchi stew, please.Gimchijjigae hana juseyo.
물 좀 주세요.Some water, please.Mul jom juseyo.
휴지 좀 주세요.Some tissues, please.Hyuji jom juseyo.
계산할게요.I'll pay / Check, please.Gyesanhalgeyo.

Short Dialogue

Staff
몇 분이세요?
How many people?
Myeot bunisseyo?
Customer
두 명이요.
Two people.
Du myeong-iyo.
Staff
주문하시겠어요?
Are you ready to order?
Jumunhasigesseoyo?
Customer
김치찌개 하나 주세요.
One kimchi stew, please.
Gimchijjigae hana juseyo.
