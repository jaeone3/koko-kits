Koko Cafe Ordering Kit
Learn how to order coffee in Korean naturally.

ROLES

Staff
Takes the order and asks whether the drink is for here.

Customer
Orders a drink, chooses takeout or dine-in, and pays.


KEY PHRASES

1. 아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요.
   One iced Americano, please.
   Aiseu amerikano hana juseyo.

2. 포장해 주세요.
   To go, please.
   Pojanghae juseyo.

3. 여기서 마실게요.
   I'll drink it here.
   Yeogiseo masilgeyo.

4. 카드로 할게요.
   I'll pay by card.
   Kadeuro halgeyo.

5. 영수증 주세요.
   Can I get a receipt?
   Yeongsujeung juseyo.


SHORT DIALOGUE

Staff:
뭐 드릴까요?
What would you like?
Mwo deurilkkayo?

Customer:
아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요.
One iced Americano, please.
Aiseu amerikano hana juseyo.

Staff:
드시고 가세요?
For here?
Deusigo gaseyo?

Customer:
포장해 주세요.
To go, please.
Pojanghae juseyo.
