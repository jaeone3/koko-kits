Koko First Meeting Kit
Practice basic greetings when meeting someone new in Korean.

ROLES

New Person
Asks basic first-meeting questions.

You
Answers where you are from and what you are learning.


KEY PHRASES

1. 처음 뵙겠습니다.
   Nice to meet you. (formal first meeting)
   Cheoeum boepgesseumnida.

2. 어디서 오셨어요?
   Where are you from?
   Eodiseo osyeosseoyo?

3. 저는 미국에서 왔어요.
   I'm from the U.S.
   Jeoneun migugeseo wasseoyo.

4. 한국어를 배우고 있어요.
   I'm learning Korean.
   Hangugeoreul baeugo isseoyo.

5. 만나서 반가워요.
   Nice to meet you.
   Mannaseo bangawoyo.


SHORT DIALOGUE

New Person:
처음 뵙겠습니다. 어디서 오셨어요?
Nice to meet you. Where are you from?
Cheoeum boepgesseumnida. Eodiseo osyeosseoyo?

You:
저는 미국에서 왔어요.
I'm from the U.S.
Jeoneun migugeseo wasseoyo.

New Person:
한국어 잘하시네요.
Your Korean is good.
Hangugeo jalhasineyo.

You:
한국어를 배우고 있어요.
I'm learning Korean.
Hangugeoreul baeugo isseoyo.
