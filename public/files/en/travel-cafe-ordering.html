Koko Cafe Ordering Kit

Learn how to order coffee in Korean naturally. Read the Korean first, then check the meaning and romanization.

Roles

Role What they do
Staff Takes the order and asks whether the drink is for here.
Customer Orders a drink, chooses takeout or dine-in, and pays.

Key Phrases

Korean English Romanization
아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요. One iced Americano, please. Aiseu amerikano hana juseyo.
포장해 주세요. To go, please. Pojanghae juseyo.
여기서 마실게요. I'll drink it here. Yeogiseo masilgeyo.
카드로 할게요. I'll pay by card. Kadeuro halgeyo.
영수증 주세요. Can I get a receipt? Yeongsujeung juseyo.

Short Dialogue

Staff
뭐 드릴까요?
What would you like?
Customer
아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요.
One iced Americano, please.
Staff
드시고 가세요?
For here?
Customer
포장해 주세요.
To go, please.
