Koko Texting Kit
Reply naturally in short Korean chat messages.

Register note: these phrases are casual (반말). Use them only with close friends or peers.

ROLES

Friend
Sends short casual messages.

You
Replies naturally in a Korean chat style.


KEY PHRASES

1. 뭐 해?
   What are you doing?
   Mwo hae?

2. 나 방금 도착했어.
   I just arrived.
   Na banggeum dochakhaesseo.

3. 조금 늦을 것 같아.
   I think I'll be a little late.
   Jogeum neujeul geot gata.

4. 괜찮아?
   Are you ok?
   Gwaenchana?

5. 나중에 연락할게.
   I'll contact you later.
   Najunge yeollakhalge.


SHORT DIALOGUE

Friend:
도착했어?
Are you here?

You:
나 방금 도착했어.
I just got here.

Friend:
조금 늦을 것 같아.
I think I'll be a little late.

You:
괜찮아. 천천히 와.
It's okay. Take your time.
