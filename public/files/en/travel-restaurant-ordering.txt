Koko Restaurant Ordering Kit
Say how many people and order food at a Korean restaurant.

ROLES

Staff
Seats guests and asks for the order.

Customer
Says the party size and orders food.


KEY PHRASES

1. 두 명이요.
   Two people.
   Du myeong-iyo.

2. 김치찌개 하나 주세요.
   One kimchi stew, please.
   Gimchijjigae hana juseyo.

3. 물 좀 주세요.
   Some water, please.
   Mul jom juseyo.

4. 휴지 좀 주세요.
   Some tissues, please.
   Hyuji jom juseyo.

5. 계산할게요.
   I'll pay / Check, please.
   Gyesanhalgeyo.


SHORT DIALOGUE

Staff:
몇 분이세요?
How many people?

Customer:
두 명이요.
Two people.

Staff:
주문하시겠어요?
Are you ready to order?

Customer:
김치찌개 하나 주세요.
One kimchi stew, please.
