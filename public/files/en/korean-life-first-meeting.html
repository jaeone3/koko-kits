Koko First Meeting Kit

Practice basic greetings when meeting someone new in Korean. Read the Korean first, then check the meaning and romanization.

Roles

Role What they do
New Person Asks basic first-meeting questions.
You Answers where you are from and what you are learning.

Key Phrases

Korean English Romanization
처음 뵙겠습니다. Nice to meet you. (formal first meeting) Cheoeum boepgesseumnida.
어디서 오셨어요? Where are you from? Eodiseo osyeosseoyo?
저는 미국에서 왔어요. I'm from the U.S. Jeoneun migugeseo wasseoyo.
한국어를 배우고 있어요. I'm learning Korean. Hangugeoreul baeugo isseoyo.
만나서 반가워요. Nice to meet you. Mannaseo bangawoyo.

Short Dialogue

New Person
처음 뵙겠습니다. 어디서 오셨어요?
Nice to meet you. Where are you from?
You
미국에서 왔어요.
I'm from the U.S.
New Person
한국어 잘하시네요.
Your Korean is good.
You
아직 배우고 있어요.
I'm still learning.
